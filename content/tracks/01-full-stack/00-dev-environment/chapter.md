Every boss fight in this path ends with "push your repository" and "run the tests". This chapter makes sure you can actually do that: navigate the **terminal**, use **git** for every change, manage packages with **npm**, and **debug** methodically instead of guessing.

You can do the quests here in the browser, but practise every command for real on your own machine as you go. Install Node.js (LTS), git and VS Code, and keep a terminal open.
