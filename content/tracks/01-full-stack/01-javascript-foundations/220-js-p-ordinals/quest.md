# Practice: Ordinal Numbers

1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th… but 11th, 12th, 13th (not 11st!), and then 21st, 22nd, 111th. A classic edge-case exercise.

## Your task
`ordinal(n)` returns the number with its English suffix, for non-negative integers.
