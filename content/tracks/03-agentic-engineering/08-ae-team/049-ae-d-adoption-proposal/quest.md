# Write-Up: Proposing Agent Adoption to Your Team

Your team of six (two juniors, three mid-level engineers and a lead) hasn't officially adopted coding agents. A few people use them quietly. Your lead has asked you for a **one-page proposal** for a 6-week pilot.

### Include
1. **The problem and the opportunity**: what you hope to improve, concretely.
2. **Scope of the pilot**: which tasks, which tools, and who takes part.
3. **Guard rails**: security, review, disclosure, and data handling.
4. **Support for juniors**: how to avoid skill atrophy.
5. **Success measures**: what you'll measure, the baseline, and the decision criteria.
6. **Risks and costs.**
