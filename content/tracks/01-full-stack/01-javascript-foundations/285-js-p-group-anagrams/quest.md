## Your task
`groupAnagrams(words)` groups words that are anagrams of each other (case-insensitive). Return an array of groups; each group keeps the words in their original order, and the groups are ordered by the first appearance of their first word.

`["listen", "google", "Silent", "enlist", "gogole"]` → `[["listen", "Silent", "enlist"], ["google", "gogole"]]`
