## Your task
`spiral(matrix)` returns the elements of a rectangular matrix in clockwise spiral order, starting top-left: `[[1,2,3],[4,5,6],[7,8,9]]` → `[1,2,3,6,9,8,7,4,5]`. Keep four boundaries (top, bottom, left, right) and shrink them as you go.
