# Practice: Leap Years & Days in a Month

A year is a leap year if it's divisible by 4, **except** centuries, which must also be divisible by 400. So 2000 was a leap year, and 1900 wasn't.

## Your task
1. `isLeapYear(year)`
2. `daysInMonth(year, month)`, where `month` is 1–12.
