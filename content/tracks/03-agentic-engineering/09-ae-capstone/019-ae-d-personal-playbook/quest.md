# Write-Up: Your Personal Agent Playbook

Bring the whole track together into **your own playbook**: the workflow you'll actually follow when working with coding agents on your capstone and beyond. It should be practical and personal: what works for you, in your stack, with your current skill level.

### Include
1. **When I use an agent and when I don't.**
2. **My standard workflow**, step by step, from task to merged PR.
3. **My prompt and spec templates** (a short version).
4. **My context setup**: what's in my CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md.
5. **My verification checklist** (short).
6. **My safety rules.**
7. **How I keep learning**: habits that prevent skill atrophy.
