# Agent Drill: Reading delivery metrics #7

The DORA metrics measure delivery at **team** level: deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate (deploys that cause incidents or rollbacks) and time to restore. Use them to see whether a new workflow (like adopting agents) actually helps.
