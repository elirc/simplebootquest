## Your task
For two arrays of primitives, return new arrays **without duplicates**, in first-seen order:

1. `union(a, b)`: everything in either.
2. `intersection(a, b)`: in both (in `a`'s order).
3. `difference(a, b)`: in `a` but not in `b`.
4. `symmetricDifference(a, b)`: in exactly one of them (the `a` part first).
