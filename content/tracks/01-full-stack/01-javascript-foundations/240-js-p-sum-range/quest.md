## Your task
1. `sumRange(a, b)` returns the sum of all integers from `a` to `b` inclusive. It works when `a > b` too (`sumRange(5, 1)` → 15).
2. `sumRangeFast(a, b)` does the same **without a loop**, using Gauss's formula: the sum of a range is `count × (first + last) / 2`.
