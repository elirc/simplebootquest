# Write-Up: Team Guidelines for AI-Assisted Development

You're the tech lead of a team of six (two juniors, three mids, one senior) adopting coding agents. Write the **team guidelines**: one page people will actually read and follow.

Cover:

- Which tasks agents are encouraged for, used carefully for, and avoided for.
- The expectations for PRs containing agent-written code.
- How juniors should use agents without stunting their growth.
- Shared assets (context files, commands, prompt library) and who maintains them.
- How you'll measure whether this is working (and not just "lines of code").
- How the guidelines will evolve.
