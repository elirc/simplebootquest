Arrays in memory vanish when your server restarts. Databases keep data safe, let many users read and write at once, and answer questions about it quickly. SQL is the language for all of that, and it's been around for 50 years for good reason.

The SQL quests in this chapter run in a real SQLite database in your browser. Your projects will use PostgreSQL. For everything taught here, the syntax is the same.
